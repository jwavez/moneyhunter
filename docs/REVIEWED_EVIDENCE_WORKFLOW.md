# Human-reviewed evidence and private outcomes

Implemented in `worker/src/evidence.js`, `moneyhunter/review_bridge.py` and the existing `evidence.py`/grading pipeline. This is not an automatic sold feed or a BUY unlock.

## Use the existing Discord menu

1. In the existing sold-prices/flip-log channel, open `/menu` → **Evidence / Private outcomes**. The first signed interaction in a known configured community channel binds the evidence workspace to that guild. Optional `EVIDENCE_GUILD_ID` can pin it explicitly; no new credential is required. Other guilds/DMs fail closed.
2. Choose **Sold observation**, **Cash quote**, **Acquisition quote** or **Active-count snapshot**, then category. Complete the exact identity, original public source URL and category attributes (`key=value; key=value`). For tech with no storage capacity, explicitly enter `capacity=not applicable`; do not invent an MPN. Repeat the same exact product name/identity across observations to join their records.
3. **Enter observation details** opens a second modal. Sold price is actual accepted GBP item price + buyer-paid postage, **excluding buyer-only fees**, not an active asking price or crossed-out best offer. Dates satisfy listed ≤ sold ≤ observed ≤ human review. Duplicate sold URLs/item IDs cannot inflate the sample. Stock/price terms must be actual verified terms, not membership/coupon guesses.
4. **Review queue** is private and requires signed Manage Server or Administrator permissions in the bound guild. Open **Original source**, inspect actual accepted price/currency, exact model/size/condition/variant, dates and absence of private data; **Review & approve** requires explicit `CHECKED` plus meaningful notes of what was inspected. This records human attestation, not independent automatic verification. Reject bad/incomplete evidence; revoke a later-discovered error. The submitting owner may also be the authorised human reviewer; no second person or assistant approval is invented.

All new steps are private/ephemeral. The old `/soldprice`/`/review` pending-list interface remains for backwards compatibility, not a separate approval/import path; the existing **Submit sold price** panel button opens the new workspace. Historical `comp:*` submissions remain pending and are not silently migrated into trusted records.

## Cash / offers / velocity

* Cash: actual cash/bank GBP amount, exit/testing/postage deductions, original source URL, exact identity/grade and original timezone-aware quote timestamp. Vouchers are rejected. Uninspected/theoretical quotes can remain pending but cannot be approved as a verified floor. An exact physically accepted/tested quote must explicitly enter `CASH; PHYSICALLY ACCEPTED` in the payment/acceptance field and record the exact grade/testing/deductions in conditions; this is a human attestation, not a guarantee that a later physical item will be accepted. A used-grade quote does not establish acceptance of a NEW acquisition; reviewers must verify that exact condition/grade relationship, not silently mix grades. Cash expires after **24 elapsed hours**, never after a newly assigned review date. CeX automatic lookup remains disabled; no challenged endpoint is retried.
* Acquisition: original public retailer source AND exact HUKD deal URL, current GBP price, known inbound delivery, original observation timestamp, actual ONLINE IN STOCK attestation. Expires after 24 hours. This can feed the existing reviewed-offer gate; it is not a checkout guarantee.
* Velocity: actual count of active exact listings and original observation date/source, with counting scope privately retained. Existing sold-sample and velocity minimums still apply. Asking depth alone never becomes selling speed. At least five sufficiently fresh sold observations with listing dates are needed for the measured sold-sample days-to-sell calculation; this is not a marketplace forecast.

## Persistence, revocation and concurrency

Draft/pending/approved/rejected/revoked records and immutable audit events persist in the existing Worker KV, separate from capture. Reviewer/user IDs and notes are private. The public `/api/reviewed-evidence` endpoint exports ONLY redacted approved exact market facts; no pending forms, audit identities, outcomes, personal costs or bankroll. It is intentionally public approved market data, not an authentication bypass or credential-bearing endpoint.

One bounded fetch per scanner run derives from the already configured capture URL. Every record is revalidated by `evidence.py` with revision/dedup checks. The bridge does **not** copy trusted community data into `evidence.json` or a durable trusted cache: read failure or withdrawal produces no community comps next scan, while unrelated native reviewed/asking paths remain. Record revisions bind published community valuations; interactive reads suppress a stale/missing/revoked community revision instead of displaying the previous price. Cash/offer-only groups remain facts counts until matching sold evidence exists; they do not manufacture a sold valuation.

KV is eventually consistent, not a transactional database or CAS store. Revision-bound human decisions reject stale forms; durable terminal vetoes prevent reject/revoke from being resurrected on the next coherent read, including a conflicting approval. Concurrent duplicate approvals cause the public facts reader to fail closed rather than count one sale twice. Audit and persistence failures do not return a success claim. Propagation can lag (normally around a minute), and an already running scan can retain its earlier snapshot until the next run; no instantaneous cross-service revocation/exactly-once guarantee is claimed. Historical messages are not edited or reposted. Storage/read limits are deliberately bounded to 400 submission records; overflow fails closed for operator review rather than dropping or silently trusting a partial table. No paid service, GitHub PAT or new secret is used. A current-facts read costs one bounded KV list plus record/terminal reads (up to roughly 2N+1 for N submissions), including interactive revision checks; this is not unlimited free capacity. No extra Browse allocation is added.

## Private actual outcomes

Choose **Private outcome** and enter your item/reference; actual bought, listed and optional sold timezone-aware ISO timestamps; GBP `buy=...; sale=...; costs=...; model_days=...` (omit sale or use zero while unsold). Costs must include actual relevant operating costs. **My outcomes** reads only the signed user's guild-scoped records. Elapsed selling days start at LISTED, not first seen or purchased. The view reports sold count, unsold/censored count, sold-sample median and median absolute model error. It is not a survival-adjusted forecast. No personal transaction or operating P&L is exported, posted to wins/XP, or silently used for FAST/sold grading. The separate pre-existing public opt-in flip log remains unchanged; do not use that for private recording. No tax/UC, bank or ledger integration was added.

Fixtures are labelled synthetic and never seeded into KV or the production evidence store. Real reviewed sales/cash/outcomes only exist after an actual human enters and reviews them; passing tests is not evidence of real sales or profitable inventory.
