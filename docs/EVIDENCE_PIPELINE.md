# Evidence-first scanner: operation and honest limits

## What this repair does — and does not do

This is an evidence/operational repair, not a new sold-data entitlement. HotUKDeals public RSS is the only automatic acquisition source. eBay Browse is active competition context only (at most four new lookups/scan, cached per query/day). It cannot establish realised prices or sale speed. No new external account, paid service, authenticated scraping or CeX-block bypass has been added. No new real comps are bundled; `data/evidence.json` starts empty. The 44 legacy CSV keys remain **reference-only**, including historical mixed-condition guide ranges. Their blank floor/velocity fields are not filled with guesses.

This intentionally means BUY alerts remain unavailable until real reviewed evidence exists. The improvement is a usable intake→valuation→acquisition→grading path plus an affordable research queue, not fake alerts or a guaranteed weekly profit.

## Intake: reviewed public evidence, not community auto-approval

1. Choose affordable online physical items from the exported `review_queue`, not by heat alone. Priority rewards product identifiers, new-to-scanner offers, known delivery and existing reviewed evidence; it does **not** predict profit. Queue length is bounded to 50.
2. Use actual sold listings/authorised exports to establish the exact product, condition, size, edition/platform, contents and variant. Use GBP UK seller-receivable gross amounts including buyer-paid delivery but excluding buyer-only protection fees. Do not convert an asking price, annual guide sales volume or a best-offer struck-through asking price into a sold observation. If the true accepted price is hidden, do not enter it.
3. Record each distinct sale URL, actual sold date, price and identical structured identity. A listing date is needed to measure days-to-sell; missing dates remain unknown. At least five fresh exact-variant observations are needed for A. Multiple sales from a single listing URL are not inferred. This is conservative sample evidence, not an extrapolated marketplace-wide sales forecast.
4. Record a contemporaneous, same-query exact-identity active listing count and public source URL for the sell-through sample. The house metric is observed sold observations / active listings ×100 (zero active uses denominator 1); it is a ratio, **not** a probability. Velocity snapshot must be ≤7 days old. Do not cherry-pick high-price fast sales or combine sizes/conditions. The reviewer must verify query completeness; schema validation cannot prove a website transaction happened.
5. For a cash floor, record the exact accepted model/contents/condition quote, GBP cash/bank amount (never voucher), source URL, date and all exit delivery/packaging/deductions as `exit_cost`. The engine subtracts those costs; floor quotes older than one day cannot qualify A. CeX quotes remain subject to testing, acceptance and price changes; no quote guarantees no loss or same-day online cash.
6. Review the exact retailer acquisition offer: its HotUKDeals `url`, public retailer `source_url`, observed item price, delivery cost, online/in-stock confirmation and identical identity. Feed titles often omit size/variant; a reviewed exact offer resolves them. Quote ≤1 day old and exact price/URL match are mandatory for signals. In-store/C&C-only, preorder and unavailable feed offers remain excluded even with an old reviewed quote. Price/stock still need checking immediately before a human purchase.
7. Put these facts in the schema below, mark `reviewed: true` only after review, and run `python -m moneyhunter.cli import-evidence input.json`. All entries validate before an atomic save; repeated imports update the same public id. Duplicate observation URLs (including across records), nonfinite/negative money, future dates, non-GBP sales, asking prices, voucher floors, incomplete identities, unreviewed records and unknown fields fail validation. URLs may contain only allow-listed public search/product parameters, never credentials/tokens. No contact, buyer, username, personal bankroll, benefits, account or tax data belongs here.
8. Review and commit `data/evidence.json` normally. Main's reviewed file is the authority on each fresh checkout; runtime state cannot silently overwrite it. The scheduled state branch publishes a copy for consumers. Community `/soldprice` submissions remain pending suggestions in the Worker's existing review inbox; there is deliberately no automatic pending→accepted bridge or bulk download of member identities.

### Schema 1 (field guide, not fabricated market observations)

Top level: `{"schema_version": 1, "records": []}`. Each record:

- `id`: stable lowercase letters/digits/underscore/hyphen, max80; `match`: specific human-readable keyword string; `category`: supported category.
- `reviewed`: boolean true; `reviewed_at`: actual ISO date.
- `identity`: string fields `condition` (`new`, `used`, `refurbished`) and `variant`, plus category fields below. Standard edition/variant should explicitly be `standard`.
- `observations`: nonempty list of `{url, sold_at, listed_at?, price, currency:"GBP", kind:"sold", identity}`. Every identity must exactly equal the record's identity. URLs must identify distinct actual transactions/listings; list dates must precede sale dates. Do not include test fixtures in production.
- `velocity` (optional): `{observed_at, active:<actual nonnegative integer>, url}`. Observations after this snapshot do not count in its ratio.
- `floor` (optional): `{amount, exit_cost, currency:"GBP", kind:"cash", observed_at, url, identity}`.
- `offers` (optional list): `{url:<exact feed URL>, source_url:<retailer URL>, observed_at, price, delivery, online:true, in_stock:true, identity}`.

Category identity fields: gaming: `platform, edition, format`; trainers/sportswear: `sku, size, gender`; fragrance: `concentration, ml`; LEGO: `set_number`; tools: `model, contents`; tech: `model, capacity`. Include full model/storage/edition/region in variant where needed. Identity equality is intentional: boxed/sealed, bundles and body-only kits are not interchangeable. A reviewer must not label a different-condition quote or transaction as matching merely to pass validation.

### Valuation and checks

Target is the lower quartile of **fresh exact-condition realised observations**; stretch is their median. Stale observations age out at 30 days, with the oldest supporting observation setting the valuation clock. Days-to-sell is the median observed listing-to-sale interval only when every supporting observation has a listing date and there are at least five. No fixed household estimate is converted into measured velocity. Floor, sold, velocity and offer clocks are independent; querying active asks never refreshes a sold date or condition.

A requires every gate. B still requires reviewed sold evidence, current exact reviewed retailer offer, known landed delivery, online eligibility, confirmed deal-page availability and the existing profit/ROI/downside checks. Missing floor or speed may qualify only VERIFY, never BUY. FAST routing requires measured ≤10-day velocity; category stereotypes no longer manufacture speed badges. Unknown checks fail closed. HUKD expiry checks are bounded to 12 financially viable reviewed offers; all graded/WATCH/price-error routes share a total 24-page budget and 8-second timeout and use live/expired/unknown; failures, exhausted budgets and unrecognised pages are unknown. Unknown WATCH/price-error checks remain retryable, never marked sent or expired. The retailer offer is a dated human check, not a real-time stock API guarantee.

All scanner money gates retain starter £200 representative bankroll / £60 landed cap / ≥£8 EV profit / ≥40% EV ROI. EV outcome weights, shipping rates and 3% returns are house assumptions, not calibrated profitability guarantees. Fees include standard UK eBay business category rates, order fee, regulatory fee and 20% fee VAT conservatively (not reclaiming VAT). Free-to-buyer outbound home-delivery postage, £0.50 packing and returns reserve are deducted. Paid inbound delivery adds to acquisition cost; unknown delivery stays unknown and cannot signal. Promotions, account-specific surcharges, subscription/insertion overhead, item-specific insurance/hazardous shipping and taxes on profits are not modelled: budget them separately. Generic tech uses the conservative fallback fee; broad gaming is not a console-specific discount. Use only platforms/accounts whose terms permit your commercial activity; a zero-fee calculator option does not establish eligibility. Never switch to private-seller fees to evade business rules.

Max landed cost is a **financial ceiling**, not approval: profit, ROI, tier cap and downside are applied; all evidence/stock checks still matter. Worker calculator buy input means total landed acquisition cost. Custom outbound postage updates both net and the financial ceiling. Worker legacy price lookup is explicitly hypothetical reference data, not fresh sold valuation.

## Operational state, reports and migration

- `scanned`: unique surviving feed offers this run, not unique opportunities for the week. Source health reports raw items, within-run duplicates, failures and old items (>7 days) dropped; unknown publication dates are separately counted.
- `new`/`rescanned`: first observed vs seen again by canonical deal URL in a 45-day public operational history. First run after this migration seeds history; "new" means new to this history, not newly published or profitable. `price_changed` tracks changes separately.
- Report separates sold-valued from legacy reference-only, delivery-known/under-cap from item-only lower bounds, rejected/unmatched and actual expiry checks/unknowns. No claim that "zero expired" means every offer is live. Zero BUY is not proof the market has no deals.
- `scan_state.json`: public hashed offer history, timestamps and active-ask cache. `review_queue.json`: actionable public research tasks, cost basis and blockers. Existing `seen.json` list and `prices.json` remain supported. New files are optional on first migration; malformed history fails visibly instead of silently deleting it. Full-title hashes replace truncated-title dedupe: legacy price-key entries age out; fresh baselines and grade-specific alert keys seed once. WATCH/trending keys remain independent, and B→A promotion can alert at the same price.
- Workflow restores operational state, tests offline, reads a typed tier index, scans, writes an Actions health summary and publishes state through a normal fast-forward commit (no forced rewriting). Failed scans retain previously published state. Reviewed source inputs remain in main, never restored over it from runtime state.
- Public tier index 0 corresponds to engine **T1 Scout**, not a lower £60 cap. Failed/missing/invalid private tier responses produce a safe warning and starter fallback, never a private response body. Fixing the private token/grant if necessary remains a separate account action.
- Public export is schema2, preserving prior keys and adding evidence/status/funnel/review fields. The state branch is now normal history, which makes later audits possible; monitor repository growth/retention. It contains public deal data only.
- `MONEYHUNTER_DISABLE_SEND=1` is a hard outbound-message kill switch, even if webhooks exist. `scan --dry-run` does not write state. Tests mock all data access and delivery; no tests use production credentials or post messages. Discord payloads suppress mentions from external titles. Failed delivery is counted/exported safely without webhook URLs; unsuccessful alerts are not marked sent and can retry on a later scan.

## Cadence diagnosis and source expansion

Observed history (London/GMT): 17 scheduled starts between 3 Nov 02:35 and 7 Nov 09:10, median scheduled-start gap 6.93h, min3.96h/max8.46h, despite 30-minute cron. Last scan itself took about26s. Evidence does not establish why individual starts were missed. GitHub officially says scheduled jobs can be delayed or dropped and a disabled workflow cannot self-resurrect. Cron is moved to minutes13/43 off the hourly rush; **this is not proof or a guarantee of repaired punctuality**. The scanner warns on previous-success gaps >90min, publishes last-success timestamps and job summaries, but cannot independently alarm during its own downtime. A genuinely punctual external scheduler/uptime monitor needs separate authorisation/setup. No watchdog loops, duplicate scheduled workflows, live dispatches or paid runtime were created.

Source expansion is intentionally not faked: Awin retailer product feeds require an account plus feed/advertiser access and may be incomplete/stale. They can supply acquisition SKU/price/stock, not resale sold evidence. No Awin access was configured, no retailer feed was represented as connected and no public endpoint/redistribution right was presumed. An eventual adapter should accept only an authorised documented feed, compare exact EAN/SKU and delivered price, preserve source timestamps and recheck stock. More deals without resale coverage are not an upgrade.

## Validation and rollout

Run `MONEYHUNTER_DISABLE_SEND=1 python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` for the standard-library core suite and `MONEYHUNTER_DISABLE_SEND=1 python -m pytest -q` for all tests including legacy pytest-style functions, then `(cd worker && node test.mjs)`. New CI runs only offline tests, never live scanner delivery. Review/merge is separate; nothing is live merely because this branch/PR exists. Scanner workflow changes take effect only after merge; Worker changes also require a separate approved deployment. Validate real source access/quotes and run a no-send controlled scan before enabling new production evidence. Consistent weekly profit is not guaranteed; track realised net, capital turnover, missed captures and evidence coverage, not message volume.


## Follow-up: independent resale lookup and populated research (7 Nov 2026)

- The export now includes `pricing_catalog` and an explicit `pricing_status`. Reviewed valuations are exported even if the product has no offer in this RSS batch. This removes an unnecessary **pricing lookup** bottleneck: a current manual acquisition quote is required for BUY/VERIFY, not to see researched sold values. It does not remove any signal guard.
- Worker Price Check consumes that catalog first, requires exact condition/variant identifiers, independently ages the actual sold date, links actual sources and flags samples below five. Missing identity or stale data does not fall through into a falsely verified quote. Empty/old-schema catalogs remain visibly reference-only; a failed feed returns an unavailable warning.
- The control panel exposes a paged **Research Watchlist** with prioritised live-scan candidates, landed-cost unknowns, exact blockers and required resale thresholds. Those thresholds are financial requirements, never asserted market prices. `/api/health` is a public read-only visibility endpoint containing scan age/provenance/coverage, not personal account data.
- `observed_at` may now be recorded per sold observation, separately from `sold_at` and record `reviewed_at`. It must satisfy sale ≤ observation ≤ review. Older records without an observation date remain supported and export unknown, never an invented observation timestamp. Optional `item_price` plus `shipping` must both be supplied and sum to `price`; `price` still excludes buyer-only protection fees. New intake should always retain the actual observation date and shipping breakdown when known.
- Search links now preserve SKU/set number, hyphenated models and platform details instead of cutting at any hyphen or after six words. Used/new/refurbished contradictions cannot be overridden by a reviewed offer. Trending breakeven includes known delivery and explicitly labels unknown delivery as an item-only lower bound. Tool resale guidance is online-delivered, not collection-only; best-offer asking is never described as a verified cash floor.
- `data/research_watchlist.json` contains three real-source research candidates (LEGO 75402, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe physical Switch, LEGO 40813), with observed retailer prices, source URLs, delivery unknowns and two **unaccepted search-index sold leads**. It is **not** an evidence import. Public retailer pages opened successfully; eBay sold pages timed out and one public read returned its own error page, so research stopped without bypassing it. Accepted sold observations remain **zero**. Best-offer hidden prices, used-vs-new comparisons and cumulative active-listing sold counts were excluded.
- LEGO 40813 illustrates why “discount” is not automatically a flip: HUKD £21.50 item-only cost needs roughly £32.66 gross just to break even, while official LEGO currently offers the same set £24.99 plus £3.95 delivery. This retail alternative is context, **not** sold evidence or a floor. Argos shows 75402 £45, with delivery/address stock still unknown; Amazon shows new Mario Kart £34.99 with conditional free-delivery wording. No BUY has been established.

Remaining intake task: verify exact public sold pages in a permitted browser, or supply an authorised sold export. Preserve actual sale date, accepted price, buyer-only fee, postage, condition, exact set/SKU/region/platform and URL; do not infer a sale date from delivery estimates or listing revisions. Marketplace Insights access remains a separately restricted entitlement; neither current Browse keys nor this repair creates it. Automated acquisition adapters can be added only for a documented authorised source; no such free broad sold-history endpoint has been established here.
