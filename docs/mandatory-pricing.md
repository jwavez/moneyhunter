# Mandatory supported resale price (8 November 2026)

Every physical product notification now needs a fresh, exact-variant supported market price, visibly displayed at the final serializer. Missing/expired/ambiguous prices stay in background research, never become a WATCH card, target-price teaser, trending entry or individual hottest teaser. Aggregate source/category health remains available. Held identities are persisted separately from delivery acknowledgements and retried through the existing bounded research allocation; no seen keys are reset.

An active-ask publication estimate requires three independent known UK NEW sellers, known competitor delivery, two supporting offers within 10% of the genuine cheapest ask, and a starting listing no higher than that cheapest ask. Risky identities need completed official getItem detail checks. This is not proof of sold value, authenticity, stock at checkout, liquidity, or BUY qualification. All existing Strong/manual-review/cap/cost gates remain unchanged. Worker additionally checks the Python exact-identity attestation is tied to the same title, query, observed time, listing and comparator records; both boundaries require the displayed sale price.

All 18 existing retail RSS feeds and the independent capture remain; eBay is complementary. Two of the four existing affordable research slots now cover supported tools, with two of four detail slots reserved. Total query quotas are unchanged. LEGO still requires a verified full product photo as well as a price.

## Cost/identity repairs

Title and description are parsed as separate delivery clauses. An optional battery £29.99 immediately before a description heading Delivery must never become £29.99 postage. Explicit separately priced secondary tool/accessory titles are reduced to their primary bare tool only when the RSS merchant price establishes its price; otherwise they are held as ambiguous. No secondary offer is invented. Older ambiguous Worker rows lose their misleading landed-cost calculation without refreshing observation times.
